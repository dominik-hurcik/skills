# 15-point checklist: contract completeness

Go through these 15 points in every contract analysis or draft. Mark each with a status:
- ✅ OK: the point is met correctly.
- ⚠️ WARNING: the point is present but needs detail or carries risk.
- ❌ MISSING: the point isn't covered, and it should be.
- ➖ N/A: the point doesn't apply to this contract (say why).

At the end, give the total: **X/15 points met**.

---

## 1. Preamble and date of signing

Does the contract have a proper date, place of signing, and context for the cooperation (a „zważywszy, że…" whereas clause where justified)?

**Check:** a full date (dd-mm-yyyy), the place, and that the preamble doesn't already contain obligations (preamble ≠ body).

## 2. Parties and representation

Are the parties' details complete (full name, legal form, KRS/REGON/NIP, address), and do the signatories have authority (KRS entry, or a dated power of attorney)?

**Red flag:** a signatory with no stated basis of authority risks invalidity.

## 3. Definitions

Are all key terms (capitalised in the text) defined? Are the definitions internally consistent and used as defined?

**Check:** „Utwór", „Informacje Poufne", „Wada", „Specjalista", „Timesheet". Mandatory in IT contracts.

## 4. Subject of the contract

Is the subject described precisely? Are exclusions clear? Is the cooperation model stated (T&M, fixed price, body leasing, fee for service)?

**Red flag:** „świadczenie usług informatycznych" (provision of IT services) with no detail makes an empty contract.

## 5. Parties' obligations

Are both parties' obligations listed? Are the client's obligations stated (providing the environment, data, access, timely answers)? Is the contract free of one-sidedness?

## 6. Remuneration and payment

Amount or rate, currency, payment term, invoicing mechanism, basis for billing (timesheet, acceptance protocol, report), late-payment interest, and an indexation mechanism for a long-term contract?

## 7. Copyright and IP

Who owns the works? Transfer or licence? Fields of exploitation listed expressly (art. 50/74 PrAut)? When do the rights pass? Is the remuneration for the transfer stated (or included in the main fee)? An anti-copyleft clause? Derivative rights? Moral rights: an undertaking not to exercise them?

**Critical for IT contracts.**

## 8. Confidentiality and NDA

Scope of confidential information (definition), duration during and after the contract, exceptions (public information, independently developed), contractual penalties for breach?

## 9. Data protection and GDPR (RODO)

Is there a data processing agreement (art. 28 RODO) where required? Who is the controller and who is the processor? Sub-processors? Duration of processing? Return or deletion at the end?

## 10. Contractual penalties

For which breaches? Amounts or percentages proportionate to the breach? Can penalties accumulate? Is additional compensation reserved (art. 484 § 1 KC)? Are there limits?

**Red flag:** penalties out of proportion to the breach. A court can reduce them (art. 484 § 2 KC).

## 11. Liability and limits

A liability cap (for example 12 months of fees)? Exclusion of lost profits (utracone korzyści)? **The intentional-fault exception** (art. 473 § 2 KC: a limit on liability for intentional fault is void)? Exclusion of indirect damage?

## 12. Term and termination

Fixed or indefinite term? Notice period (with notice, or with immediate effect)? Grounds for immediate termination? Effects of termination (settling work in progress, returning materials, lapse or transfer of IP)?

## 13. Force majeure

Is there a force majeure clause? Does it cover pandemics, cyberattacks, and sanctions? A notification procedure?

## 14. Dispute resolution

The competent court (agreed or statutory)? Mediation or arbitration as a pre-litigation step? Governing law (usually Polish, but state it expressly with EU parties)?

## 15. Final provisions

Form of amendments (written, on pain of invalidity)? Severability clause? Annexes listed and referred to in the body? Number of copies? Entry into force?
